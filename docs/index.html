Livox Mid-360 room scan

A hall of about 10 m by 21.5 m, scanned in 75 seconds by moving a Livox Mid-360 lidar around a table top. Open a viewer, then drag to orbit, scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan. Each one downloads 8–12 MB, so give it a moment.
